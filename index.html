How the estimator works

The tool compares your selected Thor model and order number with observed shipping activity and community-reported batches. It then estimates when your order may ship and shows separate delivery windows for DHL and 4PX.

The calculation updates as shipping patterns change. It does not access AYN's private order system and cannot see the official status of an individual order.

What you need

Choose the exact Thor model you ordered and enter the numerical portion of your order number. The estimate is only as useful as the information entered.

What can change the date

Production speed, model availability, holidays, customs, carrier capacity, and changes to AYN's shipping priorities can all move the final date.

Frequently asked questions

Is this an official AYN tracking tool?

No. Where Is My Thor is an independent community project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by AYN Technologies.

Does the site store my order number?

The order number is sent to the estimator service to calculate a result. This website does not intentionally use it to identify you or access your AYN account.

Why are DHL and 4PX dates different?

The carriers typically have different transit times and delivery networks, so the estimator shows a separate window for each one.

What should I do if the estimate is wrong?

Use official AYN communications as the source of truth. You can also send a correction or bug report through the Contact page.
