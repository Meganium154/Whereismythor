FAQ

Questions people usually ask

Is this an official AYN tracking tool?

No. It is an independent community project and cannot access AYN’s private order system.

Does the site store my order number?

The number is sent to the estimator service to calculate a result. It is not used to sign in to or access your AYN account.

Why are DHL and 4PX dates different?

The carriers use different transit networks and normally have different delivery times.

What should I trust if the estimate differs from AYN?

Always trust official communications from AYN over this estimator.
