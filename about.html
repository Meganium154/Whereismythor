About

A community tool built to answer one repeated question.

Where Is My Thor helps AYN Thor customers get a practical estimate of when an order may ship.

Why it exists

The same shipping questions appear repeatedly across handheld-gaming communities. This project turns publicly available information and community-reported activity into a simple estimate.

Independent project

Where Is My Thor is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or operated by AYN Technologies or its partners. Product names and trademarks belong to their respective owners.

Accuracy

Manufacturing schedules, holidays, logistics providers, customs, and other factors can change shipping times without notice. Always rely on official communications from AYN for your order status.
