About Where Is My Thor?

Where Is My Thor is an independent community project created to help AYN Thor customers estimate when their orders may ship.

The estimator uses publicly available shipping information, community reports, and observed shipping trends. It provides an approximation, not an official shipping confirmation.

Why I built this

After seeing the same question asked repeatedly across Reddit, Discord, and other handheld-gaming communities, I built a tool that could provide realistic estimates based on available data.

Independent project

Where Is My Thor is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by, or operated by AYN Technologies or any of its partners. All trademarks and product names belong to their respective owners.

Accuracy

Manufacturing schedules, holidays, logistics providers, customs, and other factors can change shipping times without notice. Always rely on official communications from AYN regarding your order status.

Support the project

This estimator is independently built and maintained. Your support helps keep it running and improve it.
